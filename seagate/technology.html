⚙️Core Technology: HAMR & the Mozaic Platform
🔥

HAMR — Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording

Foundational Technology

Seagate is the world's only manufacturer shipping HAMR drives at hyperscale volumes. A nano-laser momentarily heats a tiny spot on the disk, reducing magnetic anisotropy so the write head can flip individual bits on ultra-high-density media. The spot cools within nanoseconds, locking data in place and enabling unprecedented areal density gains.

The Mozaic Platform Family

Platform Architecture

Mozaic is Seagate's generational HAMR platform: Mozaic 3+ (3.6 TB/disk, in production) → Mozaic 4+ (4+ TB/disk, volume production 2026) → Mozaic 5+ (lab demonstrated) → target 10 TB/disk by 2028 → enabling 100 TB HDDs by 2032.

Vertically Integrated Photonics

Competitive Moat

Seagate designs and manufactures its own nanophotonic laser components in-house. This vertical integration delivers supply chain resilience, shortens customer qualification timelines, and sustains predictable manufacturing economics — a capability no competitor can replicate at scale.

Mozaic 4+ New

Mozaic 4+ incorporates a redesigned suspension architecture and an enhanced system-on-chip that enables precise recording at higher densities while maintaining enterprise-class reliability — without requiring disruptive architectural changes in existing data centers.
