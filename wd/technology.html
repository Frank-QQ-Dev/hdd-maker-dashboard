⚔️Core Strategy: Parallel ePMR + HAMR Innovation Paths
ePMR

Energy-Assisted PMR

Microwave-assisted writing on a proven PMR foundation. WD continues extracting every viable capacity gain from ePMR, with a roadmap extending to 60 TB, offering customers a fast-deploy, well-validated upgrade path.

  • Current flagship: Ultrastar DC HC590 — 26 TB
  • 40 TB UltraSMR ePMR — qualifying with 2 hyperscalers, H2 2026 volume production
  • ePMR roadmap extends to 60 TB (leveraging HAMR innovations without adding power)
  • Shared architecture with HAMR — improves manufacturing efficiency and yield
  • Customers upgrade without infrastructure re-architecture
HAMR

Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording

Laser heating overcomes the magnetic recording trilemma (superparamagnetic limit), enabling higher-anisotropy media and dramatically smaller bit sizes. WD believes the optimal HAMR inflection point is 40 TB and above.

  • HAMR HDD qualifications underway with 2 hyperscale customers
  • Volume production ramp planned for 2027
  • Roadmap: 100 TB by 2029
  • Shares common architecture with ePMR — lower BOM, better yields
  • TSA, OptiNAND, and UltraSMR innovations carried forward to HAMR platform

🎯 Why Two Paths? Customer-Controlled Technology Adoption

  • Hyperscalers and enterprises choose ePMR or HAMR on their own timelines — no forced technology transitions
  • Both paths share the same underlying architecture, giving WD manufacturing flexibility and superior yield economics
  • ePMR leads: 40 TB UltraSMR qualifications now, H2 2026 volume; HAMR follows: 2027 ramp → 100 TB by 2029
  • Overlapping technology paths deliver continuous, predictable capacity growth customers can plan around

📡

High Bandwidth Drive Technology (HBDT)

Industry First

Leveraging the precise track-following capability of the Triple Stage Actuator (TSA), HBDT enables multiple read/write heads to operate simultaneously on paired tracks. Throughput scales linearly: 2-track = 2×, 8-track = 8×.

WD demonstrated live dual-track simultaneous read/write at Innovation Day 2026. Hardware is already in customer hands for validation today.

🔀

Dual Pivot Technology (DPT)

Available 2028

A second actuator on a separate pivot at the opposite end of the drive (unlike previous vertically-stacked designs that sacrificed capacity). Distributing heads between two pivots allows reduced disk spacing, more platters, and higher capacity — while simultaneously increasing performance.

Currently in the lab; commercial availability expected in 2028.
